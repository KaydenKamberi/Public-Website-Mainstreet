Thanks for reaching out! Here's everything you need to know about getting a website for your bakery, and what we'll need from you.

## Who we are

Mainstreet Sites SD builds websites for local businesses in San Diego and keeps them up to date every month. We're Kevin and Kayden, the co-founders, and Cheyenne, our team member. We started this business to help old-fashioned businesses keep up and grow with the future.

We serve all of San Diego.

## What you get

**The Website and Monthly Management Plan:** a website built for your bakery, plus monthly management so you never have to worry about it.

**Your website will include:**

- Menu with prices, including seasonal items
- Custom cake and order info
- "Open now" status
- Reviews from Google
- Location, hours, and directions
- Meet the baker
- Call Now button that stays on screen on phones

Optional sections like offers and badges only appear if they're true for your business.

**Every month, we handle:**

- Content updates (hours, prices, photos, specials)
- Checking that your site is up and its buttons work
- Domain renewal care
- Google Business Profile updates
- A monthly report
- Up to 2 edits a month (content updates and seasonal refreshes don't count toward these)
- Seasonal refreshes (holiday hours, promotions)

## Price

- **$60** for the first month
- **$40 a month** after that
- Building your website is included in these payments. No big upfront cost.
- **Minimum:** 4 payments ($60 + $40 + $40 + $40) and 4 months. After that, you choose how long to keep going.
- **You own your website.** If you ever stop, you keep it.

## How it works

1. You send us the information below.
2. We build a demo of your website.
3. We meet you in person to show it to you.
4. If you like it, you make your first $60 payment, and we finish and launch your site.

## What we need from you

**The basics (every business):**

- Your business name, spelled exactly how you want it on the site
- Your address, or the area you serve
- Your hours
- The phone number customers should call or text
- The email where messages from your site should go
- Your logo, if you have one
- Whether you have a Google Business Profile (if not, we'll help you set one up)
- Links to your reviews on Google, Yelp, or Facebook

**For your bakery:**

- Your menu and prices
- How custom orders work: how far ahead to order, how to order, and any deposit
- Pickup and delivery options
- Allergen notes you want listed
- Seasonal or holiday items

## Photos

**What to send:**

- Your best-selling items
- Custom cakes or orders you've made
- The inside and front of your bakery

**Tips:**

- Hold your phone sideways (horizontal).
- Turn the lights on. No flash.
- Clean up first: no clutter, tools, or trash in the shot.
- No people's faces without their permission.
- Send photos by text message or email, not WhatsApp. WhatsApp makes them blurry.

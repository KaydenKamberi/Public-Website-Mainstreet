# Business Brief

## 1. Business Overview

- Business name: Mainstreet Sites SD
- Business status: Being started. The changes were made this week; no businesses have been researched for demos yet.
- Business description: Builds websites for businesses, mainly restaurants, located in San Diego.
- Why this business: Originally started to build websites for trade workers, but it was very difficult to create a process for collecting each customer's information for their website in a limited amount of time. Switched to restaurants because their information is easier to gather and they are easier to handle.
- How or where it operates: Research businesses online, build a demo website for them in advance, then pitch in person. Kevin and co-founder Kayden meet the owner or manager in person.

## 2. Target Customer

- Primary customer: Family-owned restaurants in San Diego that have no website and are not big chain-operated restaurants. The business will stick to smaller restaurants until it has more proof of its work.
- Customer need: Without a website, restaurants lose customers. Many people search online and look through a restaurant's website before visiting in person; without one, they may lose interest or decide not to make the trip.
- Why they might choose this business:
  - The business handles their website management for as many months as they choose (after the minimum term), so they don't need to worry about the website.
  - They may like that the owners are young people starting a business and be interested in seeing what comes of the work.
  - The price is acceptable.
  - They are shown a demo of what their website could look like before buying.
